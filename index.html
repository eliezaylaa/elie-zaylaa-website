01 — About

Product-minded developer, comfortable across the whole stack.

I’m based in Paris and finishing my Master’s in Information Technology at EPITECH (RNCP level 7, class of 2027). Alongside my studies I’ve worked as a freelance developer for about six years, building apps for restaurants, a pharmacy and retail clients — handling onboarding, scoping, development and delivery myself.

Lately my focus is full-stack development with AI integration: LLM APIs, retrieval-augmented generation, LangChain/LangGraph agents and MCP servers. I choose the stack, write the API, wire up the front end, and work in Scrum with pull requests when I’m on a team.

Before tech I worked in hospitality, and I tutor maths, coding and languages — so I’m used to explaining things clearly and dealing with real people under pressure.
